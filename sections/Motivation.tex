\chapter{Introductory Case Studies} \label{chapter:motivation}
\glsresetall


Here, we look at example datasets and how they can be modelled using a compositional approach. We will look at classification datasets. We begin with small toy datasets that are used for illustrative purposes only. Specifically, we will look at the Iris, Wine, and Diabetes datasets. We follow with an artificial dataset that is used to demonstrate the compositional approach for larger datasets and varying number of classification classes. We finish by looking at the models for the discretionary lane change problem using the NGSIM dataset \cite{administration_next_2016}. We explain the datasets first, then the model development, and finally the results.
